Merf Support

Get started

Open Merf and choose a folder for your scraps. Click its menu bar icon or press Control–Shift–Space to open the drawer. Choose a different shortcut in Settings if another app already uses that combination.

Capture something

Press Command–N while Merf is active to write a note. Command–Return saves it. Right-click the menu bar icon and choose Capture Clipboard for copied text, files, or images. Drop individual files onto the drop area inside the drawer, or click that area to choose files. Original files are preserved. If dragging toward the menu bar opens Mission Control, use the drawer drop area instead.

Find your files

Scraps are saved as normal files in the folder you selected. The folder button reveals the current file in Finder. Merf supports text and Markdown notes; clipboard images are saved as PNG files. Rich previews depend on macOS Quick Look and the file type.

Troubleshooting

If the drawer will not open, click the menu bar icon and check Settings for a shortcut conflict. If a folder is unavailable, choose it again. For an unsupported preview, use Open file to view it in its usual app. Ordinary subfolders are not included in the collection.

Share from Safari and other apps

Open the app’s Share menu and choose Merf, then Save to Merf. You can add a title and a note. If Merf is missing, use Edit Extensions in the Share menu and enable it. Available items depend on what the source app shares.

Shared items wait in a private local inbox until Merf is running and has access to your collection folder. If you have not chosen a folder yet, open Merf and select one. Websites are saved as links that open in your browser; Merf does not download an offline copy of the page.

Keep an email

In the 1.1 preview, drag a message from Apple Mail’s message list onto Merf’s edge pocket or drawer. Merf saves a copy of the email, with its attachments intact, and shows a text preview. Choose Open in Mail to read the saved email and access attachments. Attachments are not yet shown as separate scraps.

Drop into the edge pocket

Drag a file onto the small tab at your chosen screen edge, or anywhere over the open drawer, including the large preview. Merf copies it into your collection and preserves the original.

Search and organize

Use the Stacks menu below the drawer header to choose a stack, Inbox, or All scraps. Open Library with Command–F to search your collection. Search includes filenames, note text, PDF contents, and text recognized in images and scanned PDFs. New files may take a little time to appear in content searches, and text recognition may not find every word.

Use Keep handy to pin frequently used scraps. Use stacks to group related items without moving their files. Pins and stacks belong to Merf’s local organization settings.

A ready scratchpad and a closer look

Control–Shift–N opens the scratchpad and restores your unfinished draft. Swipe left or right with two fingers over the preview to cycle through scraps. Float a scrap into its own window to keep it alongside your work. Supported audio and video can play in the preview; use Open file when a format needs another app.

Contact

Email support@getmerfapp.com for help with Merf. Include your macOS version, Merf version, and the steps that led to the problem. Please avoid sending sensitive files.
