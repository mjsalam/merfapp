Merf Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026. Covers Merf for Mac, iPhone and iPad, including TestFlight versions. Features vary by version: Mac 1.0 does not include built-in iCloud sync or the Share extension; Mac 1.1 and iOS 1.0 include them.

Merf helps you keep notes, links, images and files. There is no Merf account, advertising, tracking or analytics SDK. We do not operate a server that receives your scraps.

Files, notes and collections

Merf reads the collection you choose and saves notes and copies of imported files there. You can use a local collection, the default Merf iCloud Drive collection, or another folder you explicitly select. Preferences, folder authorization, unfinished drafts and preview caches are stored locally on your device.

iCloud Drive

When you choose an iCloud collection, Apple synchronizes its files and Merf organization metadata using your Apple Account and iCloud Drive. Metadata includes added dates, pins and stacks, stored in a hidden .merf folder inside the collection. Your devices must use the same Apple Account and collection for synchronization. Apple handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy; the developer does not receive it. Local-only collections do not use Merf’s iCloud synchronization. Other cloud providers may synchronize folders you select under their own policies.

Search and previews

On Mac, Merf can build a local search index from file contents, PDFs and text recognized in images. Text recognition takes place on your Mac. Search indexes and preview caches are not sent to the developer. iOS currently searches filenames.

Sharing to Merf

When you choose Merf in another app’s Share menu, Merf receives only the content that app provides and any note you add. It saves copies in a private local inbox shared with its extension. Open Merf with an available collection to import those items. Imported batches are removed from the inbox; pending batches remain until imported. An iCloud destination then synchronizes the imported files. Website captures save links, not offline copies of web pages.

Clipboard and selected text

Merf reads clipboard content when you request capture or paste. On Mac, optional automatic paste into a new empty note is off by default. Selected text is received when you use the Share menu or the Mac Send to Merf Service. Merf does not continuously monitor your clipboard.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Your scraps remain until you remove them. You can manage collection files in Finder on Mac or Files on iPhone and iPad. Deleting a file from an iCloud collection also deletes it on other synchronized devices, subject to Apple’s recovery options. Choosing a local collection stops new captures from going to the cloud collection; it does not erase existing cloud files. Removing Merf does not automatically delete your collection folders or iCloud files. You can manage Merf’s iCloud access in your device’s iCloud settings. Contact us if you need help removing pending shared items or local app data.

Apple services

Purchases, App Store distribution, iCloud and platform diagnostics are handled by Apple under its applicable terms and privacy practices. Merf does not process payment card details.

Support

Contact support@getmerfapp.com for support, privacy questions or deletion requests for correspondence you have sent us. We use information you voluntarily provide to respond and retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Please do not send sensitive scraps unless necessary. We will update this policy if our practices change.

This website

This site uses no analytics or tracking cookies added by Merf. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process IP addresses and request information to provide its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
