Merf Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026. Applies to Merf 1.0 for Mac and the Merf 1.1 preview. Features identified as preview features are not included in 1.0.

Merf is a Mac utility for viewing and capturing notes, images, and files. The current version has no Merf account, advertising, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated cloud service.

Files and notes

Merf reads the collection folder you choose and saves captured notes, copied files, and images there. Your files remain ordinary files. Merf stores preferences, unfinished note drafts, folder authorization, and your selected scrap locally on your Mac.

Local search and organization in the 1.1 preview

Merf creates a local search index from your collection, including extracted text and text recognized in images and scanned PDFs. Text recognition takes place on your Mac. Merf also stores local preview caches, scratchpad drafts, pins, and stack membership. These are not sent to the developer.

Share extension in the 1.1 preview

When you choose Merf in another app’s Share menu, Merf receives the items that app provides, together with any title or note you add. It temporarily saves copies in a private local inbox shared by Merf and its extension. Merf imports these items into your selected collection folder when it is running and can access the folder. Successfully imported items are removed from the inbox; items awaiting import remain there until they can be collected. Website captures save a link rather than downloading the webpage.

Clipboard and selected text

Merf reads clipboard content when you request capture or paste. If you turn on automatic clipboard paste, opening a new empty text note can read the clipboard. This option is off by default. Merf receives selected text when you invoke Send to Merf through macOS Services. Merf does not continuously monitor clipboard content.

Sharing and cloud folders

Merf does not send your scraps to a developer-operated server. Opening, copying, or dragging a file into another application shares it according to the action you take. If you choose a folder managed by a cloud provider, that provider may synchronize the folder under its own terms and privacy policy. Merf 1.0 and the 1.1 preview have no built-in cross-device synchronization.

Apple services

Purchases, App Store distribution, and any platform diagnostics are handled by Apple under Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices. Merf does not process payment card details.

Your choices

You may disable automatic clipboard paste in Settings, discard an unfinished draft in Merf, and manage or delete your saved scraps through Finder. Removing the app does not automatically delete files in your collection folder.

Support

For privacy questions, contact support@getmerfapp.com. Information you voluntarily send for support may be used to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive scraps unless necessary to explain the issue.

We will update this policy if our data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

This website

This website uses no analytics or tracking cookies added by Merf. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may process visitor IP addresses and request information to operate and secure its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
