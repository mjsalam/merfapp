A little space for the things you need

Good things,
within reach.

A useful note. A reference image. That file you always need. Merf keeps them a keyboard shortcut away, in a quiet drawer at the edge of your Mac.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

For macOS 13 and later · No account required

A shortcut to your collection.

Choose a folder and bring it into view with Control–Shift–Space. Browse thumbnails, flip between scraps, and open or drag a file wherever you need it. Make the shortcut and screen edge your own.

Catch a thought.
Keep your flow.

Press Command–N to write a note. Capture copied text or images, send a selection through macOS Services, or collect files in the drawer. Your originals stay where they are.

In the Merf 1.1 preview

Less hunting.
More having it handy.

More ways to catch something useful, find it again, and keep it close. These features are in development for the next release; they are not included in version 1.0.

Catch it

Share → Merf.

Send a website from Safari’s Share menu, with an optional title and note. Share text, images, and files from compatible apps, too. If Merf is closed, your captures wait for its next launch.

Websites are saved as links, not offline copies.

Drop it

A pocket at the edge.

Drag a file onto the small tab at your screen’s edge. It expands to welcome the drop. Or collect files directly over the open drawer—even over the preview.

Find it

Search beyond the filename.

Find words inside notes and PDFs, and text recognized in images and scanned PDFs. Search runs locally on your Mac.

Keep it close

A place for your regulars.

Pin the scraps you reach for in Keep handy. Gather related scraps into lightweight stacks without moving the original files.

Catch a thought

A scratchpad that waits for you.

Bring up your scratchpad with Control–Shift–N. Your unfinished thought is there when you return, ready to become a note.

Stay in your flow

Flip, float, and play.

Swipe through previews with two fingers. Float a scrap in its own window alongside your work. Preview supported audio and video right in Merf.

Your files. Your folder.

Your scraps stay ordinary files in the folder you choose. No Merf account, advertising, or analytics. Version 1.0 is for Mac, with no built-in cross-device sync.
