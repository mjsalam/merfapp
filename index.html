A small drawer.

01

Give your scraps a home.

Choose a folder. Notes, reference images, documents—the files you keep reaching for, all in one place.

02

Summon at will.

A global shortcut brings Merf in from the side of your screen. Browse thumbnails, flip through scraps, open a file, or drag it into your work.

03

Catch the fleeting thought.

Command–N opens a note, ready to type. Capture copied text and images before the next distraction launches.

IN PREVIEW

More ways to bring your world into Merf. These features are in the 1.1 preview and aren’t included in version 1.0.

01

Incoming transmissions.

Share websites, text, images, and files from compatible apps. Drag emails from Apple Mail, attachments intact. Website captures save links, not offline pages.

02

A pocket on the edge.

A little tab at the edge of your screen opens to receive dropped files. The drawer accepts drops over the preview, too.

⊔
03

Find the needle. Keep the haystack.

Search notes, PDFs, and text recognized in images. Pin your regulars in Keep handy. Group scraps into stacks without moving their files.

✳
04

A thought, held in orbit.

Your scratchpad remembers unfinished ideas. Float a scrap beside your work, swipe through previews, and play supported audio and video.
