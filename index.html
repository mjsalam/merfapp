01

Give your scraps a home.

Choose a folder. Notes, reference images, documents—the files you keep reaching for, all in one place.

02

Summon at will.

A global shortcut brings Merf in from the side of your screen. Browse thumbnails, flip through scraps, open a file, or drag it into your work.

03

Catch the fleeting thought.

Command–N opens a note, ready to type. Capture copied text and images before the next distraction launches.

You pick the screen edge.

FLIGHT PLAN / 001
INCOMING!

Mail from your home planet.

Drag emails from Apple Mail, attachments intact. Share websites, text, images, and files from compatible apps. Website captures save links, not offline pages.

Even that email you keep emailing yourself.

FLIGHT PLAN / 002
DOCK HERE

Docking is now a one-step affair.

Drop files onto the little tab at your screen’s edge. It opens to collect them. The drawer accepts drops over the preview, too.

No 47-page docking procedure. We checked.

FLIGHT PLAN / 003
THERE YOU ARE

Less “where is it?!” More “ah.”

Search notes, PDFs, and text recognized in images. Pin your regulars in Keep handy. Make stacks of related scraps without moving their files.

A search party. Minus the party planning.

FLIGHT PLAN / 004
HOLD THAT THOUGHT

Keep a little thought in orbit.

A scratchpad that remembers unfinished ideas. Floating scraps beside your work. Swiping through previews. Supported audio and video, ready to play.

For ideas that arrive at inconvenient times.
